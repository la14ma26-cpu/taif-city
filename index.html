About Taif:

Taif is a beautiful city in Saudi Arabia. It is located in the mountains near Makkah. The city is famous for its cool weather and beautiful gardens. Taif is also known for its delicious fruits and colorful roses. It is a popular place for tourists to visit and enjoy nature.

Taif has many beautiful mountains and parks. It is famous for its Taif roses and rose farms. People can enjoy walking and relaxing in its cool weather. The city has many interesting places to visit. Taif is a wonderful city for families and visitors.

Top 3 Things to Do:

  1. Visit the rose farms.
  2. Enjoy the mountains and nature.
  3. Visit Shubra Palace.
